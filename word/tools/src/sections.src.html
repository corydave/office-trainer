Set up your section

Choose the lessons your class uses and add your own videos. You'll get a section key to give your students. There's no account and nothing to upload: everything is inside the key.

Edit a section you made before
It starts with OTK1. The form below fills in so you can change it, then make a new key.

1 Course

Which course is your section?

2 About your section

Students see this, for example "CSC 136-01, Spring 2027".

3 Lessons

Untick anything your section doesn't use. Students still see it, marked Not in your section, and it doesn't count toward their course certificate.

4 Your videos (optional)

Use your own video in place of a course video, or add one after any activity. Leave a lesson alone to keep the course videos.

5 Your section key

A section key changes what students see: which lessons count, and your videos. It doesn't collect anything: students' progress stays on their own devices. To grade work, use the grader.
