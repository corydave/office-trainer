Welcome. Over five weeks you'll learn Microsoft Excel by doing real work in real Excel: a budget, an invoice and grade calculator, a data report, a what-if planner and a workbook you review and publish with a partner. Each week ends with something you can keep.

## The rhythm of each week

1. **Lessons** explain one idea at a time, with short videos and quick questions.
2. **Drills** have you practice in a starter workbook. Select **Check my work** whenever you like.
3. A **skill check** asks you to show what you can do in a timed set of tasks, like the certification exam.
4. A **project** puts the week's skills to work on something of your own.

Every lesson, drill and project maps to the Microsoft Office Specialist: Excel Associate exam (MO-210). If you finish the course, you're well prepared to take it.

## Two ways to take this course

::: callout note
You'll always see which way you're taking the course at the top of the trainer panel. Both ways teach exactly the same skills.
:::

| | In a class section | On your own (practice mode) |
|---|---|---|
| **How you join** | Your instructor gives you a section code | Just start. No code needed |
| **Auto-checked work** | Counts toward your grade | Instant feedback, nothing recorded |
| **Screenshot steps** | Go to your instructor in one weekly upload | You compare yours with an example |
| **Projects** | Your instructor grades them with the rubric | You check your own work against the rubric |
| **Badges and certificates** | Earned as you go | Earned as you go, marked "Practice record" |
| **Where your work is kept** | Your instructor sees your results | Only on your own computer |

Practice mode isn't a lesser version. It's the same course with no grades attached, for anyone who wants the skills.

## Know how each activity is assessed before you start

Every activity shows a label at the top:

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Auto-checked** | The trainer looks at your saved workbook and tells you what's right and what still needs work. It checks the result, not the clicks: a total is right however you built the formula, as long as it's a formula and it gives the right answer |
| **Screenshot** | Some skills leave nothing in the workbook, such as customizing the toolbar or co-authoring with someone. For those you take a screenshot |
| **Graded by your instructor** (in practice mode: **Self-check with the rubric**) | Projects, where a person judges things like a clear layout and a sound model |
| **Practice only** | Never graded. Try it, learn from it, move on |

::: callout tip
If a check says something isn't done and you're sure it is, **save** the workbook (Excel for the web saves on its own after a moment), then select **Check my work** again. Saving makes sure the trainer's copy has your latest change.
:::

## Taking screenshots safely

Capture just the Excel window, not your whole screen, so email addresses, notifications and other tabs stay private.

- **Windows:** [[key: Win+Shift+S]], then choose **Window**.
- **Mac:** [[key: Cmd+Shift+4]], press [[key: Space]], then click the Excel window.
- **Chromebook:** [[key: Ctrl+Shift+Show windows]], then choose **Window**.

## Badges and certificates

- Finish a drill, skill check or project and you earn its **badge**.
- Finish a lesson and you get a **certificate**. The front is a traditional certificate; the back lists every badge you earned in that lesson and the course outcomes and MO-210 skills they cover. Finish the whole course for one more.
- Each certificate has a unique number. In practice mode it's your own record: it lives on your computer, and FLCC doesn't keep a copy. Save or print it if you want to keep it.

## Excel for the web or desktop Excel

Most of the course works in Excel for the web, including on a Chromebook. Some skills (importing data with Get Data, chart sheets, Scenario Manager, combining workbooks, saving as a web page) need desktop Excel. Those drills are labeled **Desktop Excel**, and campus labs have it installed.

## Your privacy

- The trainer reads only the workbook you have open, and only inside your own browser.
- In practice mode nothing about you is stored anywhere but your computer.
- Budgets, loans and grades are personal. The practice files use made-up numbers, and in your projects made-up or rounded numbers are always fine. Never put real account numbers, balances or other people's grades into a practice file or an AI tool.

## Making it work for you

- Choose your own fonts for the trainer panel in **Settings**. Atkinson Hyperlegible is the default; Arial, Verdana, OpenDyslexic and others are available.
- Everything works from the keyboard. [[key: F6]] (desktop) or [[key: Ctrl+F6]] (Excel for the web) moves between your workbook, the Ribbon and the trainer panel.
- Every video has checked captions.
- If you have an accommodation, such as extra time, tell your instructor. Skill checks can be untimed for you.
