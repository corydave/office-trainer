::: callout why
Kiosks, museum screens and training modules are presentations that people explore on their own. Building one teaches you to use motion and media on purpose, and to make navigation nobody gets lost in.
:::

## What you'll make

One presentation, `Lastname_P4_Tour.pptx`, that tours a place, a product or a process (your campus, a recipe, a game, a workplace you have permission to show). It needs:

1. **Sections** for the parts of the tour, and a **Summary Zoom** menu slide.
2. **Links or action buttons** on every content slide that go back to the menu, so nobody gets stuck.
3. **Transitions** on every slide, calm and consistent, with automatic timing where it fits (a photo slideshow part, for example).
4. **Animations** that reveal one idea at a time (lists by paragraph, a callout that appears when you talk about it), with sensible timing.
5. At least one **video** or **audio** clip (yours, a stock clip, or a screen recording), trimmed, set to play the right way, with alt text and captions or a written summary in the notes.
6. A clear **start** and **end** slide.

::: callout privacy
Record only your own screen and voice. Close email and messages before a screen recording, and get permission before you include anyone else's voice or face.
:::

::: callout ai
Optional: ask an AI tool to draft a 30-second script for your video, then record it in your own words. Note in the slide's notes what you changed.
:::

## Check before you submit

::: preflight P4-check
title: Preflight for your tour
starter: none
platform: desktop

- step: The tour has a Zoom menu.
  check: zoom.is
- step: It's organized into at least two sections.
  check: section.count min=2
- step: Every slide has a transition.
  check: transition.is slide=all
- step: There are at least three animations.
  check: anim.count slide=all min=3
- step: There's a video or audio clip.
  check: object.count kind=media min=1
- step: Graphics and media have alt text, and every slide has a title.
  check: access.is rules="alt,titles,links"
:::

## How it's graded

::: rubric P4
- P4-a | 30 | CLO6 | Animations and transitions serve the story: they reveal ideas in order, with sensible timing, and never distract.
- P4-b | 25 | CLO3, CLO2 | Navigation works: the Zoom menu, links and action buttons all go where they should.
- P4-c | 25 | CLO4 | Media is trimmed, set to play the right way, and captioned or described.
- P4-d | 20 | CLO3 | The tour runs on its own or by clicking without dead ends, and has a clear start and end.
:::
