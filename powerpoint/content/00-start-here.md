Welcome. Over five weeks you'll learn Microsoft PowerPoint by doing real work in real PowerPoint: a presentation that explains something you know, a brand template, a data story, an interactive tour and a team presentation you rehearse and deliver. Each week ends with something you can keep.

## The rhythm of each week

1. **Lessons** explain one idea at a time, with short videos and quick questions.
2. **Drills** have you practice in a starter presentation. Select **Check my work** whenever you like.
3. A **skill check** asks you to show what you can do in a timed set of tasks, like the certification exam.
4. A **project** puts the week's skills to work on something of your own.

The lessons cover every skill on the Microsoft Office Specialist: PowerPoint Associate exam (MO-310), plus a few teamwork skills the exam doesn't test. If you finish the course, you're well prepared to take it.

## Two ways to take this course

::: callout note
You'll always see which way you're taking the course at the top of the trainer panel. Both ways teach exactly the same skills.
:::

| | In a class section | On your own (practice mode) |
|---|---|---|
| **How you join** | Your instructor gives you a section code | Just start. No code needed |
| **Auto-checked work** | Counts toward your grade | Instant feedback, nothing recorded |
| **Screenshot steps** | Go to your instructor in one weekly upload | You compare yours with an example |
| **Projects** | Your instructor grades them with the rubric | You check your own work against the rubric |
| **Badges and certificates** | Earned as you go | Earned as you go, marked "Practice record" |
| **Where your work is kept** | Your instructor sees your results | Only on your own computer |

Practice mode isn't a lesser version. It's the same course with no grades attached, for anyone who wants the skills.

## Know how each activity is assessed before you start

Every activity shows a label at the top:

| Label | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Auto-checked** | The trainer looks at your saved presentation and tells you what's right and what still needs work. It checks the result, not the clicks: a list is numbered however you numbered it, as long as PowerPoint's numbering is on |
| **Screenshot** | Some skills leave nothing in the file, such as customizing the toolbar, printing or presenting. For those you take a screenshot |
| **Graded by your instructor** (in practice mode: **Self-check with the rubric**) | Projects, where a person judges things like a clear story and a readable design |
| **Practice only** | Never graded. Try it, learn from it, move on |

::: callout tip
If a check says something isn't done and you're sure it is, **save** the presentation (PowerPoint for the web saves on its own after a moment), then select **Check my work** again. Saving makes sure the trainer's copy has your latest change.
:::

## Taking screenshots safely

Capture just the PowerPoint window, not your whole screen, so email addresses, notifications and other tabs stay private.

- **Windows:** [[key: Win+Shift+S]], then choose **Window**.
- **Mac:** [[key: Cmd+Shift+4]], press [[key: Space]], then click the PowerPoint window.
- **Chromebook:** [[key: Ctrl+Shift+Show windows]], then choose **Window**.

## Badges and certificates

- Finish a drill, skill check or project and you earn its **badge**.
- Finish a lesson and you get a **certificate**. The front is a traditional certificate; the back lists every badge you earned in that lesson and the course outcomes and MO-310 skills they cover. Finish the whole course for one more.
- Each certificate has a unique number. In practice mode it's your own record: it lives on your computer, and FLCC doesn't keep a copy. Save or print it if you want to keep it.

## PowerPoint for the web or desktop PowerPoint

Most of the course works in PowerPoint for the web, including on a Chromebook. Some skills need desktop PowerPoint: Outline view, WordArt and columns, the slide master and layouts, Reuse Slides and Slides from Outline, charts and SmartArt, motion paths, trimming video, screen recordings, Zoom, custom shows and show settings, rehearsing timings, Inspect Document, passwords, embedding fonts and exporting video. Those drills are labeled **Desktop PowerPoint**, and campus labs have it installed.

## Your privacy

- The trainer reads only the presentation you have open, and only inside your own browser.
- In practice mode nothing about you is stored anywhere but your computer.
- Slides are made to be seen. The practice files use a made-up company, and in your projects use your own photos or ones you have the right to use. Don't put classmates' names, faces or contact details on slides without asking them, and close email and messages before you record your screen.

## Making it work for you

- Choose your own fonts for the trainer panel in **Settings**. Atkinson Hyperlegible is the default; Arial, Verdana, OpenDyslexic and others are available.
- Everything works from the keyboard. [[key: F6]] (desktop) or [[key: Ctrl+F6]] (PowerPoint for the web) moves between your slides, the Ribbon and the trainer panel.
- Every video has checked captions.
- If you have an accommodation, such as extra time, tell your instructor. Skill checks can be untimed for you.
